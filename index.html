The idea

People who want to buy or sell XRP leave offers on an exchange and wait. Like “I’ll buy at $2.00” or “I’ll sell at $2.10”. All those waiting offers together are called the order book. This page draws them as a picture.

The picture

  • Sideways = time. The right edge is now.
  • Up and down = price. Higher on screen = higher price.
  • Darker blue = more money waiting at that price.
  • Dark line = the real price.
  • Small circles = real trades. Green = someone bought. Red = someone sold.

Why it helps

  • A dark patch under the price = lots of buyers waiting. Price often slows or bounces there. Called a floor.
  • A dark patch over the price = lots of sellers waiting. Price often slows there. Called a ceiling.
  • Offers can be cancelled any second. Treat them as clues, not promises.
  • Hover your mouse over anything to get a simple explanation.

If you have a small account

  • Use limit orders. You pick the price. A market order takes whatever price is there, which can be worse on a thin market (that loss is called slippage).
  • Small is an advantage. Your amount is tiny next to the big piles, so you can get in and out without moving the price.
  • Few offers = fast jumps. Use a smaller amount and a stop-loss.
  • Be careful with piles that vanish. If “Gone in 3s” is high, many big offers are probably fake.
  • Fees matter more. Add your exchange fee and the price gap, and compare with what you hope to earn.
  • Your exchange may differ a little. This shows Kraken and Bitstamp. Check your own price.
  • No chart can predict the future. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

Mini dictionary

XRP: a cryptocurrency. USD: US dollars. Buy offer (bid): an offer to buy at a set price. Sell offer (ask): an offer to sell at a set price. Price gap (spread): the gap between the best buy and best sell price. Wall: a big pile of offers at one price. Level 2: all offers added up at each price. Level 3: every single offer separately. Whale: someone moving a lot of money. Liquidity: how easy it is to trade without moving the price. Imbalance: how lopsided buyers vs sellers are. Basis point: 0.01%. Market order: buy or sell right now at whatever price is available. Limit order: you choose the price and wait. Net flow (CVD): rushed buying minus rushed selling. Spoofing: placing big fake offers to fool others, then cancelling them.
