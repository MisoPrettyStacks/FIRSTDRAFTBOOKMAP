The idea

People who want to buy or sell XRP leave offers on an exchange and wait. Like “I’ll buy at $2.00” or “I’ll sell at $2.10”. All the waiting offers together are the order book. This page draws them as a picture.

The main picture

  • Sideways = time. The right edge is now.
  • Up and down = price.
  • Color = money waiting at that price. Dark blue = a little, yellow = a lot, red/white = a huge pile.
  • Thin line = the real price.
  • Bubbles = real trades by someone in a hurry. Teal = a buyer, pink = a seller. Bigger = more money.

The side panels

  • COB = offers waiting right now (green = buy, red = sell).
  • SVP = what actually traded at each price since you opened the page. Long bars = a busy price.
  • SVP Delta = buying minus selling at each price. Right/green = buyers won there, left/red = sellers.
  • Bottom bars = rushed buying (green) and selling (red) each second. CVD line = running total of buying minus selling.

Why it helps

  • A bright patch under the price = lots of buyers waiting. Price often slows or bounces. Called a floor.
  • A bright patch over the price = lots of sellers waiting. Called a ceiling.
  • Offers can be cancelled any second. Treat them as clues, not promises.
  • White notes on the chart point out the important things. Turn them off with “Notes on chart”.
  • Hover your mouse over anything for a simple explanation.

If you have a small account

  • Use limit orders. You pick the price. A market order takes whatever is there, which can be worse on a thin market (that loss is called slippage).
  • Small is an advantage. Your amount is tiny next to the big piles, so you can get in and out without moving the price.
  • Few offers = fast jumps. Use a smaller amount and a stop-loss.
  • Be careful with piles that vanish. If “Gone in 3s” is high, many big offers are probably fake.
  • Fees matter more. Add your exchange fee and the price gap, and compare with what you hope to earn.
  • Your exchange may differ a little. This shows Kraken and Bitstamp. Check your own price.
  • No chart can predict the future. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

Mini dictionary

XRP: a cryptocurrency. USD: US dollars. Buy offer (bid): an offer to buy at a set price. Sell offer (ask): an offer to sell at a set price. Price gap (spread): gap between best buy and best sell price. Wall: a big pile of offers at one price. Level 2: all offers added up at each price. Level 3: every single offer separately. Whale: someone moving a lot of money. Liquidity: how easy it is to trade without moving the price. Imbalance: how lopsided buyers vs sellers are. Basis point: 0.01%. Aggressive trade: buying or selling right now at whatever price is there. Market order: same thing. Limit order: you choose the price and wait. CVD / net flow: rushed buying minus rushed selling. Spoofing: placing big fake offers to fool others, then cancelling them. COB / SVP: current order book / session volume profile.
